## Brand & Style

The brand personality is defined by precision, high-velocity innovation, and technical authority. It targets enterprise clients and forward-thinking startups looking to leverage AI in the digital marketing space. The UI must evoke a sense of "approachable complexity"—conveying powerful technology through a clean, sophisticated interface.

This design system utilizes a **Corporate Modern** foundation infused with **Glassmorphism** and **High-Contrast** elements. It relies on deep architectural layers to represent the "black box" of AI, while using vibrant electric accents to represent the insights and energy generated by the agency's work. The aesthetic is sleek and tech-forward, favoring purposeful whitespace and structural clarity over decorative clutter.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in high-contrast transitions between deep space and pure light. The primary Navy Blue (#0D1B3E) provides a stable, professional foundation for the "Dark Mode" sections, while the Off-White (#F8FAFC) creates breathable "Light Mode" breaks that maintain readability. 

The Electric Blue accent (#2563EB) is reserved strictly for high-priority actions, interactive elements, and data highlights. This design system uses "glowing accents"—subtle radial gradients of the Electric Blue at low opacity (5-10%)—to draw attention to specific sections without overwhelming the professional tone.

## Typography

Typography is a primary differentiator in this design system. **Space Grotesk** is used for all headlines to provide a geometric, technical edge that signals innovation. It should be used with tight letter spacing for large headers to emphasize its structural quality.

**Inter** is the workhorse for all body copy and functional labels. Its neutral, systematic nature ensures that complex data or marketing copy remains highly legible regardless of the background color. Use "body-lg" for introductory paragraphs and "label-caps" for small category tags or pre-headers to establish a clear hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes a **12-column fluid grid** for desktop environments, transitioning to a single-column stack on mobile. A consistent 8px base unit drives all padding and margin decisions. 

Large-scale vertical rhythm is achieved through generous section gaps (120px), allowing the high-contrast transitions between Navy and Off-White backgrounds to feel intentional and distinct. Components should favor internal padding that creates a "roomy" feel, avoiding cramped text clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

To achieve a "sleek and tech-forward" appearance, depth is managed through **Ambient Shadows** and **Glassmorphism**. Surfaces do not use heavy, traditional drop shadows; instead, they utilize extra-diffused shadows with a slight Navy tint (#0D1B3E at 15% opacity) to feel integrated into the background.

In dark sections, depth is created by stacking lighter shades of Navy with a subtle backdrop blur (12px to 20px) and a 1px border of low-opacity Secondary White (10%). This creates a "frosted glass" effect that implies transparency and sophistication without sacrificing text contrast.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Rounded**, striking a balance between the rigidity of technical software and the friendliness of a service agency. 

- Standard components (Inputs, Buttons): 0.5rem (8px).
- Larger containers (Cards, Modals): 1rem (16px).
- Feature highlights: 1.5rem (24px).

Avoid completely sharp corners, as they appear too aggressive for a modern agency, and avoid full pill-shapes except for small status chips or tags to maintain a professional, architectural aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary CTAs are solid Electric Blue with White text. They feature a subtle outer glow on hover. Secondary buttons use a "Ghost" style: a 1px Electric Blue border with no fill on dark backgrounds, or a light Navy border on light backgrounds.

### Cards
Cards are the primary content vessel. On dark backgrounds, they use a semi-transparent Navy fill with a backdrop blur and a thin top-light border. On light backgrounds, they use solid White with a soft, diffused shadow to "lift" from the Off-White floor.

### Input Fields
Inputs are minimalist. In dark mode, they are outlined in a medium-blue stroke that glows slightly when focused. In light mode, they use a subtle grey-blue border.

### Chips & Tags
Used for categorizing AI services (e.g., "Natural Language," "Predictive Analytics"). These are small, semi-transparent pills with "label-caps" typography.

### Icons
Icons must be minimalist, thin-stroke (1.5pt), and strictly monochromatic (Electric Blue or White). They should never be multi-colored or illustrative; they function as technical symbols.

### Additional Components
- **Data Visualizations:** Use Electric Blue for primary data and a soft grey for secondary data.
- **Progress Bars:** Thin, sleek lines with a glowing "head" to represent AI processing.
